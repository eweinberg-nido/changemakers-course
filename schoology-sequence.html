What The Sequence Tells Us

Schoology mostly confirms the units in the Drive export. It also shows that students experienced the course through many separate submission tasks, while the slide decks carried the lesson-by-lesson reasoning. For the webpage version, the archive should show both: what students did each day and what they were expected to submit.
